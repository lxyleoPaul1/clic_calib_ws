\section{Prais--Winsten RTK Whitening (Stage-1)}
\label{sec:pw}

High-rate RTK (10\,Hz) exhibits serial correlation in position residuals.
Stage-1 must whiten without destroying low-frequency observability.

\subsection{Why uniform $\Sigma$ inflation fails}
Uniform covariance scaling (wrong AR(1) shortcut) inflates all frequencies and
yields $\sim$125\,mm RMSE---unacceptable (simulation audit, rejected).

\subsection{PW differenced whitening}
We apply Prais--Winsten (PW) differenced whitening: estimate lag-1 correlation
$\rho$ from RTK residuals and decorrelate successive epochs.
Results (simulation, Stage-1 antenna RMSE):

\begin{table}[t]
  \centering
  \caption{Stage-1 RTK paths (simulation).}
  \label{tab:pw}
  \begin{tabular}{lc}
    \toprule
    Path & 10\,Hz antenna RMSE [mm] \\
    \midrule
    v2 uniform AR(1) scale only & 36.0 \\
    \textbf{PW differenced whitening} & \textbf{27.3} \\
    0.5\,Hz (PW auto-off, $\Delta t\geq 0.2$\,s) & 23.7 \\
  \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

PW reduces 36.0$\to$27.3\,mm at 10\,Hz; at 0.5\,Hz independent epochs PW disables
automatically ($\Delta t\geq 0.2$\,s).
This section supports trajectory quality for Stage-2 but does not replace the
attitude-excitation requirement of Sec.~\ref{sec:mechanism}.
