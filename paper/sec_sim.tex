\section{Simulation Validation}
\label{sec:sim}

All numbers in this section are \textbf{(simulation)} unless noted.
Code, configurations, and frozen simulation baselines will be released upon publication.

We refer to simulation flight profiles as \flightD{} (wide aspect sweep with
decoupled orbit attitude), \flightW{} (POI tidal-lock dual-sector serial),
\flightJ{} (gate-off low-excitation control), and \flightY{} (insufficient yaw
diversity guard case).

\subsection{Observability gate (joint FIM $\lambda_{\min}$)}
We audit a $12$-dimensional extrinsic block $F_{\mathrm{ext}}$ (translation in
meters, rotation in radians, local $SE(3)$ parameterization).
$\lambda_{\min}$ is the smallest eigenvalue of the Schur-complement marginal
information on this block (unitless in the chosen metric).
\begin{table}[t]
  \centering
  \caption{Coplanar ablation vs.\ multi-layer (simulation).}
  \label{tab:lambda}
  \begin{tabular}{lcc}
    \toprule
    Configuration & $\lambda_{\min}$ & cond.\ $(F_{\mathrm{ext}})$ \\
    \midrule
    Multi-layer (well observed) & \textbf{1.07357} & $\sim 9\times 10^2$ \\
    Coplanar (degenerate) & $\sim 10^{-12}$ & $\sim 10^{300}$ \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The coplanar $\lambda_{\min}$ is at machine-precision null ($\mathcal{O}(10^{-12})$);
multi-layer flight restores a well-conditioned margin ($\lambda_{\min}\gg 0.003$
gate).
Distinct from aspect bias--translation collinearity
(Sec.~\ref{sec:mechanism_identifiability}): this audit diagnoses \emph{geometric}
rank deficiency (Sec.~\ref{sec:mechanism_coplanar}).

\subsection{Two-stage pipeline sanity (sphere-target configuration)}
On the sphere-target sanity configuration (independent of board-free flights), the
two-stage pipeline converges to centimeter-level translation error in 50/50 trials;
mean $|t_{LW}|$ error is 6.556\,mm (simulation).
