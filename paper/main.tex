\title{Board-Free Multi-LiDAR Extrinsic Calibration for UAV-Anchored Roadside Sensing:\\
Active Attitude Excitation as a Necessary Condition}

\author{Anonymous Authors}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Board-free extrinsic calibration of roadside LiDARs from a UAV body is governed not
by sensor noise but by \emph{flight attitude diversity}.
The empirical body centroid is an aspect-dependent bias vector field on the relative
bearing sphere $u_B$; under concentrated aspect it becomes indistinguishable from
extrinsic translation and silently pollutes estimates ($\sim$211\,mm in simulation),
while active 3D attitude excitation restores identifiability and decimeter-level
accuracy ($\sim$20\,mm in simulation).
We present a separable two-stage estimator with per-LiDAR independent Stage-2 solves
for zero-FOV-overlap diagonal deployment, an aspect gate that falls back when
excitation is insufficient, and a relative-extrinsic covariance analysis showing
coherent RTK bias cancels in the relative frame (0.166$\times$ in simulation, scoped).
Field validation on a diagonal dual-Ruby intersection is structured but pending.
\end{abstract}
