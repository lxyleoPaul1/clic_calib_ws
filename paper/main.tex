\title{Board-Free Multi-LiDAR Extrinsic Calibration for UAV-Anchored Roadside Sensing:\\
Active Attitude Excitation as a Necessary Condition}

\author{Anonymous Authors}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Roadside and V2X deployments increasingly rely on multiple LiDARs with little or no
field-of-view overlap, anchored by UAV RTK trajectories rather than calibration targets.
We show that board-free body-centric calibration is governed not only by sensor geometry
but by \emph{flight attitude diversity}: the empirical body centroid forms an
aspect-dependent bias vector field on the relative bearing sphere $u_B$.
Under concentrated aspect this field degenerates with extrinsic translation and silently
pollutes estimates ($\sim$211\,mm in simulation); active 3D attitude excitation
(POI tidal-lock flight) breaks the degeneracy and restores decimeter-level accuracy
($\sim$20\,mm in simulation).
We present a separable two-stage estimator with per-LiDAR independent Stage-2 solves,
an aspect gate with centroid fallback, and a relative-extrinsic covariance analysis under
coherent RTK bias (0.166$\times$ in simulation).
Field validation on diagonal dual-Ruby intersection data is structured but pending.
\end{abstract}
