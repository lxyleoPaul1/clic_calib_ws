\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Continuous-time and spline-based calibration}
Continuous-time trajectory representations coupled with batch factor-graph
optimization are standard for multi-sensor extrinsic calibration
\cite{furgale2013unified,rehder2016kalibr}.
Our Stage-1 trajectory fit follows this lineage but \emph{separates} trajectory
estimation from per-sensor Stage-2 extrinsic refinement, avoiding cross-sensor
optimization coupling that can leak state between LiDAR solves.

\subsection{Roadside, V2X, and multi-LiDAR systems}
Infrastructure sensing at intersections motivates long-baseline, low-overlap
multi-LiDAR layouts \TODO{cite: roadside V2X multi-LiDAR calibration}.
Prior work often assumes overlapping views or shared calibration targets.
We address the diagonal-post regime ($\sim$70\,m baseline, negligible FOV overlap)
with UAV-RTK anchoring and per-sensor separable solves.

\subsection{Target-based vs.\ targetless calibration}
Target-based LiDAR--camera and LiDAR--IMU calibration remains the industrial default
\TODO{cite: target-based LiDAR extrinsic calibration survey}.
Targetless methods exploit scene structure or motion constraints; body-centric
board-free calibration uses the vehicle hull as a primitive without a physical board.
\textbf{Distinction:} we do not propose sphere targets as an alternative pipeline;
spheres are reserved for field ground-truth evaluation only (Sec.~\ref{sec:field}).

\subsection{UAV-assisted and cooperative calibration}
UAV platforms with RTK positioning enable mobile anchoring of roadside sensors
\TODO{cite: UAV RTK-assisted sensor calibration}.
Related trajectory-matching approaches align sensor frames via shared motion
\TODO{cite: trajectory-matching extrinsic calibration}.
Our contribution is orthogonal: even with a perfect RTK trajectory, board-free
calibration fails without sufficient attitude excitation---a \emph{flight-design}
requirement not captured by trajectory matching alone.

\subsection{Relation to prior work on degeneracy}
Anonymous prior work on coplanar multi-LiDAR setups identifies \emph{geometric rank
deficiency} in joint Fisher information when sensors and the body path are nearly
coplanar (simulation $\lambda_{\min}\approx 0$ vs.\ multi-layer $\lambda_{\min}=1.074$)
\TODO{cite: prior coplanar multi-LiDAR FIM degeneracy}.
\textbf{This paper addresses a different degeneracy:} aspect-concentrated body-centroid
bias aligns with extrinsic translation ($\sim$211\,mm silent pollution in simulation),
which joint lever-arm optimization cannot remove (Sec.~\ref{sec:mechanism_identifiability}).
The two failure modes are complementary: coplanar geometry vs.\ aspect--attitude
bias--translation collinearity (Sec.~\ref{sec:mechanism_coplanar}).
