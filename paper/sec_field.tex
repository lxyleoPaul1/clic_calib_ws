\subsection{Sphere-anchor evaluation protocol (method, not calibration)}
On the \textbf{same} flight segment:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Survey sphere centers (total station) $\Rightarrow$ ground-truth extrinsic ruler.
  \item Run board-free calibration once (sole method).
  \item Report $\Delta$trans, $\Delta$rot, center-reg vs.\ sphere-derived truth.
\end{enumerate}
Spheres measure error; they do not replace board-free.
